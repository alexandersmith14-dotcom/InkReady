# Brand — mimics thesolidink.com

Pulled directly from the live site (2026-08-20).

## Colors

| Role | Hex | Usage on Solid Ink |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#000000` | Nav bar, hero background |
| Off-white | `#f4f4f4` | Body/content background |
| White | `#ffffff` | Text on black |
| Orange | `#ff6700` | Promo strip / CTA accent — reuse as the compliance-alert/status color |

## Typography

- Font: **Abel** (condensed sans-serif, Google Fonts). Fallback stack: `Abel, Oswald, 'Bebas Neue', sans-serif`.
- Headlines: bold, all-caps, oversized numerals for emphasis.
- Body: same font family, no serif mixing.

## Layout notes

- Dark header/nav, high-contrast product photography style (swap for status/severity imagery in this tool's context).
- Off-white content sections against the black nav for contrast.
- Orange reserved for urgency — in Solid Ink that's promos/CTAs, here that maps to compliance flags/alerts (upcoming deadlines, non-compliant status).
