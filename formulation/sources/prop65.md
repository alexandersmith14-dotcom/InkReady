# California Prop 65 (OEHHA)

State-level heavy metal/carcinogen list. Structured list published by OEHHA. California is a large enough single-state market to track independently even though it's not federal.

## Fetch approach

OEHHA publishes the Prop 65 list in a structured, more machine-readable form than the EU/FDA sources — lowest-effort source to automate first.
