# commerce/

Rules on *selling and shipping* tattoo ink, distinct from what's legally allowed in the formulation. No single government feed covers this — it's a curated checklist, reviewed and updated manually on a schedule rather than auto-diffed.

Relevant to Solid Ink specifically since they're both manufacturer and distributor, shipping to 190+ territories.

See [checklist.md](checklist.md) for the full list, organized by category.
