# Sale/commerce compliance checklist

Last reviewed: 2026-08-20

## Registration & importer obligations

- [ ] **REACH Title II** — EU importer registration duty if importing >1 tonne/year, separate from Annex XVII formulation compliance.
- [ ] **MOCRA facility registration + product listing** — required for anyone selling under their own brand in the US, not just formulators.

## Sale restrictions

- [ ] **Professional-only sale** — some jurisdictions restrict ink sale to licensed tattoo artists/studios, blocking direct-to-consumer. Check per-market, not uniform.
- [ ] **Age-restricted sale** — applies in some states/countries, same logic as other restricted chemicals.

## Cross-border shipping

- [ ] **Hazmat classification** (DOT/IATA/IMDG) — some pigments classify as dangerous goods for transport, independent of formulation legality.
- [ ] **Customs/HS code + import duty** — per-destination, affects landed cost and clearance.

## Labeling & documentation

- [ ] **CLP labeling** (EU) — substance classification/labeling duty, distinct from the Annex XVII restriction itself.
- [ ] **SDS provision** — safety data sheet required for B2B sale to studios/distributors in most markets.

## Consumer protection (if selling direct-to-consumer online)

- [ ] **Distance-selling rules** (EU Consumer Rights Directive) — returns/cooling-off period.
- [ ] **EPR / packaging waste fees** (EU) — several member states charge sellers for packaging waste (bottles/tubes).

## Notes

- Solid Ink's site (as of 2026-08-20) shows no visible compliance claims (REACH/MOCRA/ingredient disclosure) — real gap, since MOCRA registration is now mandatory, not optional marketing.
- UK REACH diverged from EU REACH post-Brexit — do not assume UK obligations mirror the EU checklist above.
