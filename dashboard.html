New Zealand — EPA Group Standard

New Zealand · formulation restriction

Tattoo and Permanent Makeup Substances Group Standard 2020 (HSR100580, amended 2022-11-24) — in force. Concentration limits on PAHs, heavy metals, aromatic amines (<5ppm), colouring agents (<0.1% by weight). Separate regulator from Australia's AICIS — was missed in original project scoping.
